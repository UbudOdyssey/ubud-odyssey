Tour Overview

Explore Bali’s Southern Coastline

This private South Bali tour is perfect for travelers who love beaches, cliffs, sunset temples, and cultural performances.

Tour Highlights

Places You Will Visit

Experience the perfect combination of culture, nature, spirituality, and local flavors in the heart of Bali.

Melasti Beach

A beautiful beach surrounded by dramatic limestone cliffs and turquoise ocean views.

Padang Padang Beach

A small iconic beach known for clear water, white sand, and hidden cliff access.

Uluwatu Temple

A cliffside sea temple with one of the most stunning sunset views in Bali.

Kecak Fire Dance

A traditional Balinese performance at sunset with ocean and cliff scenery.

Jimbaran Seafood Dinner

Enjoy a romantic seafood dinner by the beach after sunset.

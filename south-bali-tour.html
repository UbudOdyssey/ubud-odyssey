FEATURED STOPS

A Few Places Along the Way

A relaxed route through South Bali. Stops can be adjusted depending on your interests and the time available.

Melasti Beach

Turquoise water, dramatic limestone cliffs and one of South Bali's most beautiful coastal settings.

Uluwatu Temple

Visit one of Bali's iconic sea temples, perched high above the Indian Ocean.

Kecak Fire Dance

Experience one of Bali's most famous traditional performances as the sun sets over Uluwatu.

Jimbaran Bay

End the day by the beach with an optional seafood dinner and relaxed evening atmosphere.

DISCOVER RURAL BALI

Rice Terraces, Quiet Roads & Sunset.

Travel beyond Bali's busier areas and spend the day surrounded by rice terraces, mountain landscapes, traditional villages and peaceful countryside.

Your journey finishes at Tanah Lot, one of Bali's most iconic sea temples, where the coastline becomes especially beautiful around sunset.

A Few Places Along the Way

Jatiluwih Rice Terraces

Walk through Bali's UNESCO-listed rice terraces and enjoy wide views of rural landscapes.

Batukaru Temple

Discover a peaceful mountain temple surrounded by tropical forest and a quieter Balinese atmosphere.

Bali Countryside

Travel through scenic village roads, rice fields and landscapes away from Bali's busiest tourist areas.

Tanah Lot Temple

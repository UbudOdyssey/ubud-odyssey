DISCOVER EAST BALI

Temples, Water Gardens & Mountain Views.

East Bali feels different from the island's busier southern areas. The roads become quieter, Mount Agung dominates the landscape and historic temples and royal gardens shape the journey.

This is a private and flexible journey. Your route can be adjusted depending on your pickup location, travel pace and the places you'd most like to experience.

Journey Information

  • Duration: Approx. 9 - 10 Hours
  • Start Time: Early Morning Recommended

FEATURED STOPS

A Few Places Along the Way

A scenic route through East Bali's temples, royal gardens and quieter landscapes.

Lempuyang Temple

Visit the famous Gate of Heaven with Mount Agung rising behind one of Bali's most recognizable temple views.

Tirta Gangga

Walk through a former royal water garden filled with fountains, ponds, stone sculptures and tropical greenery.

Taman Ujung

Discover a peaceful heritage palace surrounded by water, gardens, hills and distant ocean views.

East Bali Landscapes

Enjoy mountain roads, rice fields, villages and coastal scenery as part of the journey itself.

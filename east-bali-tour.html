Tour Overview

Discover the Beauty of East Bali

This private East Bali tour is perfect for travelers who want to visit Bali’s most photogenic temples, royal gardens, and scenic coastal areas in one full-day journey.

Enjoy a comfortable private car, local driver, and flexible itinerary designed for a relaxed Bali experience.

Tour Information

  • Duration: 09 - 10 Hours
  • Start Time: Early Morning Recommended
  • Pickup: Hotel / Villa
  • Price: From IDR 850K / Car
  • Type: Private Tour
Tour Highlights

Places You Will Visit

Experience the perfect combination of culture, nature, spirituality, and local flavors in the heart of Bali.

Lempuyang Temple

Visit the famous Gate of Heaven with Mount Agung in the background.

Tirta Gangga

A beautiful royal water palace with ponds, gardens, and traditional Balinese architecture.

Taman Ujung

A peaceful heritage palace surrounded by water, gardens, and mountain views.

Virgin Beach

A quiet white-sand beach on Bali’s eastern coast, perfect for relaxing after temple visits.

East Bali Scenic View

Enjoy peaceful countryside, rice fields, hills, and ocean views along the route.

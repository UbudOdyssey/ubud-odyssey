Mountains, Lakes & Waterfalls.

FEATURED STOPS

A Few Places Along the Way

A nature-focused journey through Bali's mountain lakes, forest roads and tropical northern landscapes.

Ulun Danu Beratan

Visit Bali's iconic lake temple, surrounded by cool mountain air and the peaceful scenery of Lake Beratan.

Handara Gate

Stop along the highland road for one of Bali's recognizable gateways, framed by lush mountain scenery.

Twin Lakes

Take in panoramic views over Lake Buyan and Lake Tamblingan from Bali's northern highlands.

Banyumala Waterfall

Walk into lush tropical forest and discover one of North Bali's beautiful twin waterfalls and natural pools.

OPTIONAL STOP

Want Another Highland View?

Wanagiri Hidden Hill can be added depending on your travel pace, pickup location and how much time you'd like to spend at the waterfall.
